A lightweight CMS for Next.js App Router applications, built around content files that live directly in your Git repository. It adds just enough structure for editing, organisation, and publishing without introducing a database, external content platform, or unnecessary complexity.

Useful enough to support real content workflows, but lightweight enough to stay out of the way.
