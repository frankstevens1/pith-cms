# Security

## Reporting a vulnerability

Report vulnerabilities through GitHub's private vulnerability-reporting flow. Go to the
repository's **Security** tab and use **Report a vulnerability**. Do not open a public issue.

Include enough detail to reproduce the issue — describe the affected component, conditions,
and impact. Do not include tokens, private keys, session cookies, or full content in the
report.

A maintainer will acknowledge the report within 5 business days and keep you informed of
progress toward a fix.

## Disclosure policy

Pith follows coordinated disclosure. Once a fix is merged and published, we publish an
advisory through GitHub's advisory system. We aim to release fixes for the current supported
version within 10 business days of confirmation.
